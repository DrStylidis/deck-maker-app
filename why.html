Why

I'm CEO and co-founder of Intended Future. Most weeks I'm presenting our products to clients, pitching to investors, or reporting to the board. I'm also a scientist, and if you've ever sat through a scientific conference, you know exactly what those slides look like. I was no exception.

I can lose days to a single presentation. Not to the content: I can write, and I like writing. I can make the images I want in Midjourney or Nano Banana, and I like that too. What costs me the days is layout and form. Where things sit. How much air they need. Why the third bullet refuses to line up with the second.

So a professional designer built Intended Future a proper brand book. It worked, for a while. Then the products evolved, the pitch changed, new slides were needed, and I was back at midnight nudging text boxes by two pixels.

That's when I decided I wanted an app where I drop in new text (drafted with Claude, then edited until it sounds like me), drop in the images I made myself, and get back a finished deck in Intended Future's style. Or Apple's style. Or whatever style I feel like that morning.

I coded the prototype on the way to the Design 2026 conference in Dubrovnik.

Thank you, Lufthansa, for WiFi on board.

What you're downloading is exactly what I use myself. I hope it will be useful to you too.

Kostas Stylidis
CEO and co-founder, Intended Future
