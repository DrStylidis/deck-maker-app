# Deck Maker

**Notes in. Deck out.** Turn Markdown, a PowerPoint, or a PDF into a clean, on-brand, responsive deck — preview on any device, export a PDF or PPTX, all on your machine.

Download the desktop app below. It's local-first and brings your own API key — no accounts, nothing leaves your Mac except calls to the provider you configure.

## What it does

- **Author** — drop Markdown, a `.pptx`, or a `.pdf` and it's mapped onto on-brand layouts automatically.
- **StyleMaker** — drop a PPT/PDF that carries your brand (or point at a website, or just describe it) and reuse that look as a Style across every deck.
- **Export** — one click to a pixel-perfect PDF or PPTX.
